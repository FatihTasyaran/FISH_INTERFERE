\section{Preliminary Findings}
\label{sec:findings}

\noindent
\textbf{Model:} From the replay FISH extracts one host, 88 executors, 241 nodes,
3824 entities and 3832 callbacks, joined by 6179 callback-layer interactions.
The model is served by FISH-eye, a browser-based viewer that renders it at any
granularity the expansion operator allows (Fig.~\ref{fig:screens}): from the
namespace grouping of the whole application down to the GPU work of a single
callback.

\noindent
\textbf{Setup:}
Autoware Universe runs on ROS~2 Humble on an Intel i7-12700H --- six
performance and eight efficiency cores --- with an NVIDIA RTX~A1000. The input
is Autoware's sample map and sample rosbag, thirty seconds of recorded driving
replayed at half speed. Turbo and SMT are disabled so that frequency scaling and
thermal drift do not enter the comparison, and a cgroup gives the six
performance cores to the application and six efficiency cores to the tracing
daemons: on a hybrid processor the scheduler is otherwise free to migrate a
callback onto an efficiency core, and the observer would compete for the very
cores it is measuring. Each of the three modes --- untraced, LTTng, LTTng with
\texttt{nsys} --- is repeated three times, and every value reported here is
taken from the steady-state phase of Section~\ref{sec:method}.

\noindent
\textbf{Cost:}
Table~\ref{tab:overhead-aw} reports what the instrumentation costs on the values
Autoware's own nodes publish. End-to-end latency grows by 8.7\,\% under LTTng
and 10.6\,\% with GPU profiling on top, and the ranges over the three runs do
not overlap the baseline, so the difference is not run-to-run noise. The GPU
inference the model exists to describe is the least affected. What does not move
is the work done: the perception pipeline keeps producing detected objects at
the same rate, no centerpoint cycle is missed, and no topic state monitor leaves
\textsc{ok} in any of the nine runs, so the added latency stays below what
Autoware's own supervision treats as a fault. Run without the core partition the
same two diagnostics stay at zero and end-to-end latency costs 7.7\,\% and
7.9\,\% instead, the application being free to spread over every core it finds.
Table~\ref{tab:occgrid} gives the distribution behind the one metric that falls.
