## Why
The Wu & Shi survey paragraph on ROS 2 intra-process communication (notes/mention_in_paper.txt #1) raised the question
whether FISH sees the take_shared / take_ownership choice and the intra-process enable per entity. It did not: only the
waitable registration (fish_rclcpp_waitable_init) and the dynamic publish/take events existed. The decision is made in
rclcpp's IntraProcessManager (intra_process_manager.cpp, compiled into librclcpp) → library-side hooks, no app rebuild.

'Mixed' edges, resolved the same evening (analysis_mixed_<session>/fish_tasks.v3.*): the by-order fallback had two causes,
both in the extractor, none in the data. (1) A take whose dispatch started > 5 ms later (TAKE2START; preempted or

instance identified by address with one publisher (concatenate_data's timer path) was re-paired by order with the other

## Model/paper statements this enables
- per intra edge: delivery path (IPM-matched vs DDS-in-process), buffer kind, ring depth, copies per publish — static,
  from init events, for any binary (library-side).
- Autoware's intra-process path is zero-copy everywhere; 14 of 143 subscriptions take ownership.
- the survey's "same process ⇒ same executor" and the implicit "same process ⇒ intra" are both wrong in practice; FISH
  found a same-process DDS edge in Autoware (and our own earlier rule had it wrong).

## Is the address identity safe when a pointer is shared or reused? (checked on fish_20261019_170239)
- Sharing is the norm: of 1,842 taken addresses 1,362 were taken by ≥ 2 consumers (up to 13 over an address's lifetimes;
  a concatenated cloud goes to its 4 subscribers unchanged). Each consumer's take is its own event (waitable, ts), paired
  with the last publish of that address before it → one publish instance, N edge instances; no ambiguity. (The one place
  sharing WAS an ambiguity, state links keyed by (process, address), was fixed on 10-05: key (process, subscription, address).)
- Reuse: 2,837 addresses re-published (21,036 publishes). "Last publish before the take" is exact by rclcpp's memory
  rules: a message cannot be freed (so its address cannot be reused) while any subscriber's ring buffer or callback still
  holds it; a buffer that overwrites it (keep_last) never takes it. Data: 0 consumers took an address more often than it
  was published; the next re-publish after a take comes ≥ 50 µs later (median 217 ms).
- Ordering: the publish hook fires BEFORE ipm->do_intra_process_publish (publisher.hpp), the take hook after the consume;
  0 takes precede the first publish of their address, min take − publish 8 µs.
- Remaining ways an intra pair could be non-exact: an ownership copy (IPM copies for all but the last unique_ptr subscriber:
  the copy has another address → order fallback) — none in Autoware, now known statically from fish_rclcpp_ipm_match; a
  take at the window edge whose publish is outside the look-back (1 today). DDS identity (writer GUID + source timestamp,
  ns) could only collide on two writes of one writer in the same ns (not observed: 40,666/40,666 exact).
